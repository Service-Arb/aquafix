# westernrooter.com — terms stated as terms

**Verified proof on page (grade A):** 4,700+ reviews. "Emergency" ×21, "24/7" ×5,
"licensed" ×7, "same day" ×7.

## The mechanic

Flat-rate pricing, **"no hidden fees"**, and **free estimates** are stated as contract
terms rather than marketing adjectives, and repeated at every scroll depth. 24/7 staffing
is asserted with the phone number adjacent every time, so the claim and the action are never
separated.

Agency roundups credit this site with a service request completable in under two minutes and
a callback inside five. (Grade C — asserted, not verified by us.)

## Taken

- Repetition discipline: our phone number appears in the emergency bar, header, hero, guarantee band, closing CTA and footer — six times, always next to a claim about answering it.
- The "no hidden fees" idea, pushed to its logical end: our price table footer reads
  *"Call-out is $89 and is credited in full… That is the entire price list — there is nothing else."*
- Two-minute request → our 3-field form and the *"90 seconds. No obligation, no call-out fee."* subhead.

## Rejected

- Free estimates as the lead offer. A free estimate still leaves the price unknown, which is
  the actual fear. We anchor a *number* instead and credit the call-out against the work.
