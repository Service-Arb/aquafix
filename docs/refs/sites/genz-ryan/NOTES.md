# genzryan.com — guarantee as a product

**Verified proof on page (grade A):** 8,613 reviews. "Financing" ×9, "guarantee" ×7,
same-day ×2, warranty ×2.

## The mechanic

The guarantee is packaged and named — a **"No Breakdown Guarantee"** with its own badge
asset and its own coupon page. A named guarantee is memorable, quotable on the phone, and
can be referenced in a review. An unnamed one is just copy.

Financing is surfaced above the fold rather than buried in a footer, which pre-empts the
sticker-shock objection on high-ticket jobs before the price is ever seen.

Agency roundups credit this site with reviews, same-day service, lifetime guarantees and
financing all visible above the fold, and a "Book Appointment" button locked into the
header. (Grade C.)

## Taken

- **Naming the guarantee: "The Aquafix Guarantee"**, with its own full-bleed navy band and three numbered pillars.
- Financing pre-empted in the FAQ rather than the hero — our ticket sizes are lower, so it is an objection to close, not a lead offer: *"Finance is available over $2,000 through a third party and we do not mark up the rate."*
- CTA locked into the header at all scroll depths (desktop header + mobile fixed bottom bar).

## Rejected

- Coupon-led framing. Discounts undercut the "the price is the price" position we are building; a coupon implies the list price was negotiable.
