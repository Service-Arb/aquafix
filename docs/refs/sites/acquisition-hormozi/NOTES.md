# acquisition.com — doctrine source (Hormozi)

Not a design reference. Here as the canonical public statement of the offer mechanics the
whole page is built on.

**Note on the capture:** acquisition.com is a client-rendered SPA; `curl` returns only
loading spinners. `capture.md` is therefore a rendered transcript, not a static fetch.

**Verifiable claims on the page (grade B — self-reported):** portfolio scaled to $250m+
annual revenue; Gym Launch and Prestige Labs sold to a PE sponsor for $46.2M; $100M Offers
and $100M Leads each 1M+ copies; 12M+ social audience.

## The doctrine applied

**The value equation.** Value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood of achievement) ÷
(time delay × effort and sacrifice). Three of those four terms are addressed structurally
on our page:

```
  perceived likelihood  ↑   guarantees written as terms the company pays
  time delay            ↓   "fixed today", 43 min avg arrival, 96% same-day
  effort & sacrifice    ↓   3-field form, 90 seconds, no call-out fee, no obligation
```

**Risk reversal is the cheapest lever.** Every Aquafix guarantee is phrased as a liability
we accept, never as an adjective about ourselves:

| Never | Always |
|---|---|
| "quality you can trust" | "12-month workmanship warranty, parts included" |
| "we respect your time" | "2-hour window, or the call-out is free" |
| "honest pricing" | "written flat rate before we start — the number never moves" |

**The naming rule.** Hormozi names offers so they can be repeated. Ours: **The Aquafix
Guarantee**, three numbered pillars, its own full-bleed band.

## Rejected

The infomercial register — urgency timers, scarcity counters, "BRAND NEW" flags. Those work
for information products and destroy trust for a trade that has to enter your home. We took
the offer structure and left the pressure tactics.
