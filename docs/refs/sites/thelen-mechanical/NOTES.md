# thelenmechanical.com — form in the fold, financing forward

**Proof on page:** financing ×5, same-day, on-time, emergency each ×1. Thin signal (grade C/D).

## The mechanic

Lead form visible **without scrolling**, family-centred imagery, and service icons that cut
mobile cognitive load. The financing emphasis mirrors Genz-Ryan: pre-empt sticker shock
before the number appears.

## Taken

- **Form in the fold.** Our hero is a two-column split with the 3-field quote card occupying the right half at 1440 — no scroll, no click, no modal.
- Mobile cognitive load: on the 390 layout the form is a single stacked card immediately under the hero, with the fixed bottom bar giving a second, always-available path.

## Rejected

- Stock family imagery. It is the most-recycled asset class in the trade and reads as a
  template. Our credibility comes from named crew and published numbers instead.
- Icon-per-service. Icons add scan speed but carry no proof; we spent that space on a
  from-price under each service name, which does both.
