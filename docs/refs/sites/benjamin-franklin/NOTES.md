# benjaminfranklinplumbing.com — the punctuality penalty

**Verified proof on page (grade A):** 119,705 reviews (network-wide). "On time" stated 3×.

## The mechanic — the single best thing in this folder

> "If we're not on time… **we pay you $5.00 for each minute we're late, up to 60 minutes
> (or $300).**"

The guarantee is a **number the company pays**, with a cap, stated publicly. It is not
"we respect your time". It is a liability they took on, in dollars, in writing. That is why
it is believable, and believability is the whole job of a guarantee.

The brand position ("The Punctual Plumber") is downstream of the guarantee, not the other
way round. The promise came first; the slogan describes it.

## Taken

Directly produced our arrival guarantee: **"2-hour arrival window, or the call-out is free."**
Same structure — a specific window, a specific penalty, paid automatically.

Also produced pillar 02 wording: *"Late by a single minute and the $89 call-out is waived
automatically — you do not have to ask, and you do not have to argue."* The "without asking"
clause is ours; it closes the obvious follow-up objection about having to chase the refund.

## Rejected

- Curated 5-star review wall. See the reviews decision in the parent README.
- Franchise-scale review counts are not available to a 4-person shop; our proof leans on
  arrival time and same-day rate instead, which a small operator can actually own.
