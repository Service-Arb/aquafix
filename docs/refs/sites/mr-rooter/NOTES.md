# mrrooter.com — price certainty as the entire position

**Proof on page (grade B):** national franchise; 25 star-rating instances; "upfront" ×3,
"licensed"/"insured" ×7.

## The mechanic

> "At Mr. Rooter Plumbing, **you'll know exactly what you're paying upfront**."
> "**Flat Rate Pricing** … keeps you in control with no surprises. Before starting, we
> explain the services…"
> "**No overtime**" charges.

Price certainty is not a feature in a list — it is the brand's whole reason to exist. Note
the *"before starting"* — the commitment is anchored to a moment in time, which is what
makes it checkable.

"No overtime charges" is a second-order move: it kills the specific fear that a job running
late becomes the customer's bill.

## Taken

- The moment-anchor: *"a written flat rate **on your doorstep**, before a single tool comes out of the van."*
- The overtime kill, restated as ours: *"If it takes us three hours longer than we estimated, that is our problem — you pay the number on the sheet."*
- Objection card 1 is built on this: *"We price the job, not the hour."*

## Rejected

- **The tagline "There's a reason they call us Mr."** — says nothing, proves nothing,
  occupies prime space. Logged in the parent README as an anti-pattern.
- Saying "upfront pricing" without publishing prices. We publish the table.
