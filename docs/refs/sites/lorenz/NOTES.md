# lorenzplumbing.com — process transparency reduces anxiety

**Proof on page:** "on time" ×5, licensed ×2, insured ×2. Modest signal set (grade C/D).

## The mechanic

Roundups credit this site with a video hero showing **real trucks**, years in business, and
review volume above the fold, plus benefit-led copy and clearly separated services so the
visitor never has to ask "do they even do this?".

The underlying principle is the one worth keeping: **anxiety, not ignorance, is the blocker.**
A homeowner is not deciding between vendors on features; they are deciding whether to let a
stranger into their house. Showing the process and the physical reality of the business
lowers that, and no amount of feature copy does.

## Taken

- **"The people who will be in your house"** — the crew section, with names, years on the tools and individual licence numbers. Four faces, stated as the entire company.
- The three-step "How it works" band: call → arrive in window → approve price, then fix. It exists purely to make the next hour predictable.
- Service separation: eight named services with anchor prices, so "do they do this?" is never a question.

## Rejected

- Video hero. It costs load time, and the same anxiety is answered faster by a text guarantee and a named person. Revisit only with real crew photography.
